# Payment Bank Assets

Last reviewed: 2 October 2026.

## Scope

Payment bank choices remain controlled by backend capabilities. The dropdown displays local WebP logos for BCA, BNC, BNI, BRI, BSI, CIMB Niaga, Danamon, Mandiri, Maybank, Muamalat, OCBC and Permata. Unknown codes or failed images retain the bank name and a generic icon. Asset provenance and retrieval dates accompany the files.

The control supports arrow keys, Home/End, Enter/Space, Escape, Tab, typeahead, pointer selection, outside dismissal and disabled state. Brand marks keep their original proportions and colors in a fixed white image area on either application theme.

Update this document when bank assets, source provenance or dropdown behavior changes.
